I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Astrophysical and Cosmological Relativity (ACR) group at the [Albert Einstein Institute](https://www.aei.mpg.de/) in Potsdam, working with Jonathan Gair. My position is supported by the ERC grant *"Making Sense of the Unexpected in the Gravitational-Wave Sky"*.

My research is about getting reliable science out of long-lived gravitational-wave signals, especially **extreme-mass-ratio inspirals (EMRIs)** observed by the space-based detector [LISA](https://www.lisamission.org/). I build inference methods that stay robust when waveform models and data are imperfect, and I use them to look for environmental and beyond-GR effects around massive black holes and for electromagnetic counterparts of these sources.

I completed my Ph.D. in Physics at the [National University of Singapore](https://www.nus.edu.sg/) in 2026, advised by [Alvin J. K. Chua](https://www.physics.nus.edu.sg/faculty/chua-alvin-jk/). [More about me →]({{ site.baseurl }}/about/)

<h2 class="section-title">Research interests</h2>

<div class="interest-grid">
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Robust inference for LISA</h3>
    <p>Semi-coherent and loosely-coherent analyses of years-long signals that hold up against waveform and data systematics.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Environments &amp; beyond-GR</h3>
    <p>Measuring accretion disks, other astrophysical environments and modified gravity with EMRIs, one source at a time or across a population.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Multimessenger EMRIs</h3>
    <p>Quasi-periodic eruptions and other repeating nuclear transients as electromagnetic counterparts of LISA sources.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Waveforms &amp; software</h3>
    <p>Core developer of <a href="https://github.com/perturber/StableEMRIFisher">StableEMRIFisher</a> and contributor to <a href="https://github.com/BlackHolePerturbationToolkit/FastEMRIWaveforms">FastEMRIWaveforms</a>.</p>
  </div>
</div>
